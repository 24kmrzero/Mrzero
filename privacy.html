Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 August 2026

Information collected

We collect account details such as name, email, WhatsApp number, country and trading experience. When you purchase a course, we also collect transaction references and payment receipts.

How information is used

Information is used to create accounts, review payments, unlock courses, provide support, communicate class changes and secure the platform.

Storage and access

Account records are stored in Supabase. Payment receipts and course resources are stored in private buckets protected by access policies. Administrators can review records needed to operate the service.

Sharing

We do not publish your payment receipt or private account details. Information may be shared only when required to provide the service, comply with law or protect platform security.

Retention

Records may be retained for account, payment, dispute and legal purposes. You may contact support to request correction of inaccurate profile information.

Security

Reasonable technical controls are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong password and report suspicious activity promptly.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@24kexcellence.com.
